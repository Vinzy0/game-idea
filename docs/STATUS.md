## Current Milestone

**Phase 5 — AI DM Prototype is COMPLETE as of 2026-08-11.**

The next development phase is **Phase 6A — Always-on 32x32 Board**.

## Planned Phase 6 Direction

The accepted planning direction is now an always-visible 32x32 board with persistent linked scenes and exploration/combat on the same scene. The decision-complete plan is in `docs/PERSISTENT_WORLD_IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF.md`; none of that pivot is implemented yet. Its initial IndexedDB design intentionally stores one complete `WorldSaveV2` snapshot behind a persistence adapter. Revisit storage sharding at more than 50 discovered scenes, a save larger than 5 MB, or measured p95 save time above 100 ms.
